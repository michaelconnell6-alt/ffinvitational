Scoring & Play
01
Net Stroke Play

Score minus course handicap. Best net total wins.

02
Breakfast Balls

Allowed on the first tee only. However, if you take one, you must play your second ball.

03
OB Stakes Option

Play 4 in the fairway at point of entry, or replay 3 from the tee.

04
Unmarked Woods

All unmarked wooded areas are played as hazards.

05
Ball in the Hole

Always. No gimmes, no exceptions.

06
Quad Bogey Max

Applies for the first three days. Pick up and move on.

No Gimmies.
No Exceptions.
